Questions owners ask.

Do I have to change my POS?

No. Your POS, rota, bookings and accounts stay as they are. Back of House works beside them and puts what they hold on one screen.

Can it change anything in my systems?

No. It only reads. Links to your accounts, POS and card payments can read your figures and cannot change a thing in them.

Who checks the findings?

A person at Back of House checks every line before it reaches you. Then you mark each one right, wrong or already knew.

Does anything get sent without me?

No. No message goes to a guest, a supplier or your staff until a named owner or manager says send, for that exact message.

What does it cost?

The audit is £250 plus VAT, and only if we find you £250. After that, £100 a location a month, plus VAT.

How do I stop?

30 days’ notice, by one message. You keep your reports and an export of your records, free.

What do I send for the audit?

Downloads from your POS, rota, accounts and bank. On the call you get a one-page list of exactly what to download for the systems you run.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Staff see their own shifts and hours on their own phone and clock in and out there. They see nobody else’s.

Who sees my data?

You and the people you name. It is kept apart from every other venue and never sold or shared with suppliers, drinks brands or lenders.

Is it live with venues yet?

Not yet. It is built and tested on simulated venues, and we are looking for the first three.
