Questions owners ask.

Do I have to change my POS?

No. Your POS, schedule, bookings and accounts stay as they are. Back of House works beside them.

How are the findings checked?

The platform reads each finding against your venue’s own records and checks the sums before it reaches you. Then you mark each one right, wrong or already knew.

What do I send for the audit?

Downloads from your POS, schedule, accounts and bank. On the call you get a one-page list of exactly what to download for the systems you run.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Staff see their own shifts and hours on their own phone and clock in and out there. They see nobody else’s.

How do I get started?

Book an audit. We take on a few venues at a time, because each one is set up by us, with you, so it starts with a call to agree a date.
