Privacy notice: Back of House

For visitors to bohhq.io, people who ask for a demo, people at venues we talk to, and the owners, managers and staff who sign in to the Back of House app. Draft 1.2, 4 November 2026.

Who we are

Back of House is a trading name of Back of House Drinks Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, number 14482942. We make a back-office app for pubs, bars and restaurants, and a person here checks the work each week.

Privacy contact: hello@bohhq.io. Our director works from Spain.

Two different roles

This notice covers the data we decide how to use. For that data we are the controller.

It does not cover the data a venue keeps in our app: its staff, rota, clock-in records, pay rows, guests, bookings, consent records, mail drafts, stock photos and accounts. For that data the venue is the controller and we are its processor. We act only on the venue's instructions, under a written contract. The venue's own privacy notice explains how it uses that data. See "If you work at, or visit, a venue that uses Back of House" below.

The assistant in the app. The app has an assistant that answers questions typed by a venue's users. It is automated software, not a person, and it says so on screen. It answers from the venue's own figures using fixed rules, and it makes no decision about anyone. No decision about you is made by software alone.

Who we share it with

  • Our hosting provider, Railway, which runs the app and stores sign-in records and demo requests.
  • Our backup storage provider, which holds encrypted copies; it cannot read them, because we keep the key.
  • Our business email provider (Google), which delivers demo-request notifications and our email.
  • Our website host, GitHub, which serves bohhq.io.
  • Our domain and DNS provider, Namecheap, which also forwards email sent to our bohhq.io addresses.
  • Our accountant and the tax authorities (HMRC; and in Spain, if our advisers say so), for our own tax and accounts.
  • Professional advisers, and the police or a regulator where the law requires.

Where it is kept

The app, its sign-in records and demo requests are today in a United States region of our hosting provider. The provider, Railway Corporation, is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and its UK Extension, which the UK treats as adequate (The Data Protection (Adequacy) (United States of America) Regulations 2023) and the EU treats as adequate for certified organisations. We are moving the app to the provider's EU region (Amsterdam) before any venue's real data is loaded. After the move, the Data Privacy Framework still covers the provider's US staff and tools.

Our director works from Spain. Our own records and encrypted backups are held on our own equipment there. The UK treats EEA countries as adequate for data protection. Our email provider and our website host are in the United States.

Your rights

You can ask us to: give you a copy of your data; correct it; delete it; restrict how we use it; give it to you or another business in a common format (where we rely on contract or consent); and stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests. For direct marketing, we will always stop.

Email hello@bohhq.io. We may ask for proof of who you are, but only as much as we need. We reply without undue delay and within one month. We can extend that by up to two further months for complex or many requests, and will tell you within the first month if we do.

If you work at, or visit, a venue that uses Back of House

The venue is responsible for your data in our app. Its own privacy notice tells you what it holds and why. Please send requests to the venue. If you send a request to us about data a venue holds in our app, we will pass it to that venue within 2 working days and tell you we have done so. We help the venue answer it with the tools in our app. We do not answer for the venue, because the decision is theirs.

If you work at a venue, the app checks your location only at the moment you press Clock in, Clock out or a break button, and keeps only whether you were at the venue and how far away the phone said you were: never your position or a map. Your venue's staff notice explains it.

Complaints

Please tell us first: hello@bohhq.io. We acknowledge a complaint within 30 days and tell you what we have done (Data Protection Act 2018, section 164A).

You can also complain to a regulator: - United Kingdom: the Information Commission (formerly the Information Commissioner's Office): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. - Spain: the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos: www.aepd.es. - Elsewhere in the EU: the data protection authority where you live or work.

Cookies

See our cookie policy (bohhq.io/cookies.html). In short: the website sets none. The app sets only what is needed to keep you signed in and to remember the language and display you chose.

Changes

We will post any change here with the date. If a change matters to customers, we will email the account contact first.
