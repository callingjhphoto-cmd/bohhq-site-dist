Privacy notice: Back of House

For visitors to bohhq.io, people who ask for a demo, people at venues we talk to, and the owners, managers and staff who sign in to the Back of House app. Draft 1.1, 2 November 2026.

Who we are

Back of House is a trading name of Back of House Drinks Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales, number 14482942. Registered office: Flat 8 Stepney City Apartments, 49 Clark Street, London E1 3HS.

We make a back-office app for UK pubs, bars and restaurants, and a person here checks the work each week.

Privacy contact: hello@bohhq.io, or write to the registered office above.

Two different roles

This notice is about the data we decide how to use. For that data we are the controller.

It is not about the data a venue keeps in our app (its staff, rota, clock-in records, guests, bookings, mail, stock photos and accounts). For that data the venue is the controller and we are its processor. We only act on the venue's instructions, under a written contract. The venue's own privacy notice explains how it uses that data. See "If you work at, or visit, a venue that uses Back of House" below.

Marketing to business contacts. We may email people at venues about our own service. If you are a sole trader or a partnership, we will only do that with your consent or under the "soft opt-in" in the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003, regulation 22 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/regulation/22). Every email says who we are and gives a free way to say stop.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use advertising or tracking services. We do not use anyone's data to build profiles for other businesses.

Who we share it with

  • Our hosting provider, Railway, which runs the app and stores the sign-in records and demo requests.
  • Our business email provider, which delivers demo-request notifications to our inbox.
  • Our website host, GitHub, which serves bohhq.io.
  • Our domain and DNS provider, Namecheap.
  • Our accountant and HMRC, for our own tax and accounts.
  • Professional advisers (for example our solicitor), and the police or a regulator where the law requires.

Where it is kept

  • The app, its sign-in records and demo requests are today in a United States region of our hosting provider, under the UK–US data bridge. The provider, Railway Corporation, is certified under the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which the UK treats as adequate (The Data Protection (Adequacy) (United States of America) Regulations 2023, SI 2023/1028). We are moving the app to the provider's EEA region (Amsterdam) before any venue's data is loaded. After the move, the data bridge still covers the provider's US staff and tools.
  • Our director works from Spain. Our own records and encrypted backups are held on our own equipment there. The UK treats EEA countries as adequate for data protection.
  • Our website host is in the United States, and is also certified under the UK–US data bridge.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your data;
  • correct it;
  • delete it;
  • restrict how we use it;
  • give it to you or another business in a common format (where we rely on contract or consent);
  • stop using it where we rely on legitimate interests. For direct marketing, we will always stop.

Email hello@bohhq.io. We may ask for proof of who you are, but only as much as we need. We reply without undue delay and within one month. We can extend that by up to two further months for complex or many requests, and will tell you within the first month if we do (UK GDPR Articles 12 and 12A).

No decision about you is made by software alone.

If you work at, or visit, a venue that uses Back of House

The venue is responsible for your data in our app. Its own privacy notice tells you what it holds and why. Please send requests to the venue.

If you send a request to us about data a venue holds in our app, we will pass it to that venue within [2] working days and tell you we have done so. We help the venue answer it, using the tools in our app. We do not answer for the venue, because the decision is theirs.

Complaints

Please tell us first: hello@bohhq.io. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you what we have done (Data Protection Act 2018, section 164A — https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2018/12/section/164A).

You can also complain to the Information Commission (formerly the Information Commissioner's Office): https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/ or 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

See our cookie notice. In short: the website sets none. The app sets only the cookies needed to keep you signed in.

Changes

We will post any change here with the date. If a change matters to customers, we will email the account contact first.
