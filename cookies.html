Cookie notice

Back of House Drinks Ltd · for bohhq.io and the Back of House app · Draft 1.1, 2 November 2026.

A cookie is a small file a website saves in your browser. "Local storage" is a similar store in your browser. The law treats both the same way. A site needs your consent to store or read them, unless they are strictly necessary for a service you asked for, or fall within another exception the law sets out.

The law: the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003, regulation 6 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/regulation/6) and Schedule A1 (https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2003/2426/schedule/A1), as changed by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 from 5 February 2026. "Strictly necessary" is Schedule A1, paragraph 4. The law's own examples include "automatically authenticating the identity of the subscriber or user".

The website, bohhq.io

No cookies. No local storage. No analytics. No advertising or tracking. No third-party scripts or fonts.

  • The site loads only its own pages, style sheets, scripts and fonts. Nothing is fetched from anyone else.
  • Its scripts do not use cookies, local storage or session storage.
  • The "Request a demo" button opens a form on demo.bohhq.io. That page is part of the app (below), and it is open without signing in.

The company that hosts the website logs your IP address for security purposes. That is not a cookie. Our privacy notice explains it.

Offline copy of the app's pages

If you add the app to your phone's home screen, your browser keeps a copy of the app's own pages, styles, scripts, fonts and icons, so it opens quickly. It never keeps venue data, sign-in pages, files or anything from the data side of the app.

Do we need a consent banner?

Not while everything above stays true. Everything is either strictly necessary or a display setting you chose. We review this whenever we change the website or the app.

If we ever add analytics, or anything else that is not strictly necessary, we will switch on a consent banner first:

  • Nothing optional runs until you choose.
  • Two equal buttons, "Accept" and "Reject". No boxes ticked for you.
  • A link to this notice, listing each optional cookie, its purpose, who sets it and how long it lasts.
  • A "Cookie settings" link in every footer, so you can change your mind at any time.
