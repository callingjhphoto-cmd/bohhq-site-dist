Security at Back of House

Back of House Drinks Ltd · bohhq.io/security.html · 2 November 2026

You are trusting us with your staff's pay, your guests' allergies and your inbox. This page says how we look after them, in plain words.

Your data stays yours

  • You decide what goes in and how long it stays. We act only on your written instructions, under a data processing agreement we sign before any of your data arrives.
  • We never sell your data, never compare you with other venues, and never use it to build anything for anyone else.
  • No supplier, drinks brand or other venue ever sees it.

How the app protects it

  • Sign-in. Passwords are stored only as a scrambled one-way value. Too many wrong tries locks the login for a while. Owners can turn on a second step: a six-digit code from an authenticator app.
  • Sessions. You are signed out after a period of no use that you choose, and you can sign out every device at once.
  • Who sees what. Each role (owner, manager, staff) sees only its own screens. Staff see their own shifts and hours, nobody else's.
  • A record of who looked. Every time someone opens pay details, a guest's allergy notes, an email body or clock-in detail, the app records who, when and what. You can read that record. It is built so that editing it would show.
  • Encrypted in transit. Every page is served over HTTPS only.
  • No card numbers. We never take, store or see payment card numbers.

Your people's rights

  • From one screen you can find everything held about one person, give them a copy, and erase it, with a receipt. Records the law says you must keep (payroll, tips) are kept for their minimum period and then removed.
  • Old data is cleared on a schedule you control.

Where it is kept

Today the app runs with a hosting company in a United States region, under the UK–US data bridge, and holds sample data only. Before any venue's data is loaded we move it to the company's European region (Amsterdam). Our full list of suppliers is in the sub-processor list we give you before you sign.

If something goes wrong

We tell you within [24] hours of knowing, with what we know, so you can meet your own legal deadline.
